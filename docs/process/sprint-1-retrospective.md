# Sprint 1 Retrospective

Held on the last day of the sprint, once the work is ready to submit. About 30 minutes. Write the minutes during or straight after the meeting, and record what people actually said, including disagreements.

## How to run it

1. Each member writes, on their own, two or three notes under each heading: went well, did not go well, ideas.
2. Read them out and group similar notes.
3. Pick the two or three problems that matter most.
4. For each, agree one action with an owner and a date. An action is something a person does, not a wish.
5. At the next retrospective, start by checking these actions.
