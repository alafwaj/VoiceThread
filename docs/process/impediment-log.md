# Impediment Log

Kept by the Scrum Master of the current sprint. An impediment is anything that stops or slows a team member and that they cannot remove alone: a missing answer, a tool that will not install, an absent teammate, an unclear requirement.

Rules:

- Add a row when the impediment is raised, usually at a stand-up. Do not rebuild the log at the end of the sprint.
- Record real impediments only. An empty log is acceptable; an invented one is not.
- A row stays open until its "Resolved on" date is filled in.
