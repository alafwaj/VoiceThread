# Sprint 1 Planning

Sprint 1 covers Assignment 1: requirements, initial design, and working as an agile team. Due October 16, 2026.

The minutes below are written during or straight after the real planning meeting, by the member who runs it. Record what was actually agreed.

## Suggested agenda (about 45 minutes)

1. Confirm roles: who is Scrum Master this sprint, and the order of rotation for Sprints 2 and 3.
2. Agree the sprint goal in one sentence.
3. Go through the cards on the board. Each member takes one package card.
4. For each card: what "done" means, and what it depends on.
5. Agree the stand-up days and time (at least two a week) and whether they are in person or online.
6. Agree the dates for the three board screenshots and for the retrospective.
7. List questions for the Product Owner (the instructor).
